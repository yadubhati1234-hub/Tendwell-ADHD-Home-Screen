## What Tendwell is

Tendwell is a home-screen app with a time bar, a Now card with a timer, a daily check-in, a short list of essential apps, and a pause before apps you choose to slow down on. The app is free. **Tendwell Pro** is an optional paid upgrade that adds focus hours, weekly insights, and a pause on more than 3 apps.
