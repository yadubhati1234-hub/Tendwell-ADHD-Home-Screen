# Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING]

This policy covers Tendwell (package app.tendwell), an Android home-screen app published on Google Play by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). It explains what the app collects, why, and what stays on your phone.

## The short version

- There is no account and no server of ours. Your tasks, check-ins, app lists, timers and usage counts are stored only on your phone.
- We never sell your data and never show ads.
- We use Google Firebase to receive crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, so we can fix bugs and see which features are used.
- Purchases of Pro are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

## Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your "one thing today" answers and Now task | To show them on your home screen |
| The apps you choose as essential or paused | To build your home screen and the pause screen |
| How often you open paused apps, and at what times | To show "7th open today" and weekly insights |
| Focus hours and bedtime settings | To run the time bar and focus hours |

### Calendar (optional)

If you turn on the calendar mode of the time bar, the app asks for permission to read your calendar. It reads the time of your next event on your phone to show how long you have until it. Calendar data never leaves your device. Without this permission, the time bar counts down to the bedtime you set.

### Usage access (optional)

If you turn on Usage access in settings, the app reads which apps you open from Android's usage statistics, so counts include apps opened from notifications or other apps. This data is processed on your phone only and is never sent to us.

### List of installed apps

As a home-screen app, the app reads the list of apps installed on your phone so it can show and launch them. This list is not sent to us.

## Data sent to third parties

### Crash reports and usage statistics (Firebase)

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. They collect:

- Crash logs and device details (model, Android version, app version).
- Anonymous events about which features are used, for example "pause screen shown" or "Pro trial started". These events never include your task text, your calendar, or the names of apps you open.
- An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase.

This data is sent encrypted to Google and kept for up to 90 days for crash reports and up to 14 months for analytics. Google's use of it is described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Purchases (Google Play)

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We only receive confirmation that a purchase is active; we never receive your payment details.

## Your choices

- You can turn off calendar access and usage access at any time in the app's settings or in Android settings.
- You can delete all data the app holds by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.
- To ask about or delete data held by Firebase, contact us at the address below.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
