# Delete your Tendwell data

This page covers **Tendwell: ADHD Home Screen** (package app.tendwell), published on Google Play by **db10apps**. Tendwell has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is how to delete each kind of data.

## Data on your phone

Your tasks, check-ins, app lists, focus hours, timers and open counts are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.

To delete them, do one of these:

- Uninstall Tendwell.
- Or open Android **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **Tendwell**, then **Storage**, and tap **Clear storage**.

They are deleted straight away and nothing is kept.

## Crash reports and usage statistics

Tendwell sends crash reports and usage statistics to Google Firebase. They are linked to a random app ID, not to your name, email or Google account. They never include your tasks, your calendar or the names of apps you open.

To have them deleted:

1. In Tendwell, open **Settings** and tap **Copy analytics ID** near the bottom.
2. Email [db10apps@gmail.com](mailto:db10apps@gmail.com) with the subject "Delete my Tendwell data" and paste the ID.
3. Within 30 days, we delete the crash reports, usage events and app ID linked to it, and we reply to confirm.

Without the ID we can't tell which data is yours, because Tendwell has no accounts.

**What is kept:** only totals already counted into anonymous reports, such as how many people used focus hours in a week. These can't be traced back to anyone.

**Without a request**, crash reports are deleted after 90 days and usage statistics after 14 months.

## Purchases

Purchases of Tendwell Pro are handled by Google Play, and we never receive your payment details. Your purchase history is part of your Google account. Google explains how to manage it in [your Google account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/).
